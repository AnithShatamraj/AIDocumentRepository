# Intelligent Legal Archive
Now that we have built, the AI Document Repository, using this app as my foundational platform, I want to built various apps on it. My first app is Intelligent Legal Archives (Suggest a better name).

## Purpose
Build a smart archive for the Legal business to manage their documents effectively, providing them to store their documents in a well organized manner, and smartly. They would have various document repositories (A high level document categories). 

| Knowledge Store            | Purpose                                      | Examples                                      |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Matter/Case Repository     | Documents related to a specific legal matter | Pleadings, contracts, emails, evidence        |
| Legal Knowledge Repository | General legal knowledge                      | Law books, acts, regulations, commentaries    |
| Case Law Repository        | Historical judgments and precedents          | Supreme Court judgments, High Court decisions |
| Template Repository        | Reusable legal documents                     | NDAs, employment agreements, notices          |
| Research Repository        | Internal legal research                      | Research memos, opinions, notes               |
| Client Repository          | Client-specific information                  | Engagement letters, KYC, billing              |
| Compliance Repository      | Regulatory documents                         | Policies, compliance manuals                  |
| Evidence Repository        | Evidence collected for litigation            | Images, videos, recordings                    |
| Archive Repository         | Closed matters                               | Completed litigation, expired contracts       |
| Administrative Repository  | Firm operations                              | HR, Finance, SOPs                             |


These vairious repositories will have sub categories.

When a new Matter/Case comes up AI will assit to effectively solve the matter with the help of Knowledge, Historical case data, and the general LLMs.
