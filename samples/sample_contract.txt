MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between
Northwind Traders ("Client") and Globex Consulting LLC ("Provider"), the parties
hereto.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Expiration Date: December 31, 2026

1. SERVICES. Provider shall provide professional consulting services as described
in one or more Statements of Work.

2. CONTRACT VALUE. The total value of this Agreement shall not exceed $250,000.00
over the initial term.

3. PAYMENT TERMS. Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days
of receipt (Net 30).

4. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of the State of Delaware.

5. RENEWAL TERMS. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year
terms unless either party provides sixty (60) days written notice of non-renewal.

6. TERMINATION CLAUSE. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach
upon thirty (30) days written notice if such breach remains uncured.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
